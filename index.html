From Belarus to America: a relative we found generations later

Hello, my name is Andrey.

Many times as a child I heard that my great-grandmother’s brother had left Belarus for the United States and never came back. Sometimes he sent letters, and once he sent a letter with a photograph. The photograph survived, but the envelopes and addresses did not. I knew only his name.

I asked Vitaly to help find this relative.

To my pleasant surprise, Vitaly asked a series of precise questions that all touched this person in one way or another. Some of them at first seemed unrelated to the search. That surprised me, until it became clear that every answer was a piece of the puzzle that had to be assembled to find him.

From the first facts — his name and his mother’s name, my great-grandmother — we reached a much larger result.

I learned the year he emigrated to the United States, where he arrived, where he is buried, the family he made, and the names of his wife and children.

I even saw, with my own eyes, scans of historical documents with handwritten entries: which ship, which year and which day he arrived in the United States of America.

Now I have a small dream and a goal: one day to visit this relative’s grave here, in the United States.

Andrey, USA

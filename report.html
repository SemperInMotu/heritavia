Example: Paleśsie / Charužka line.

Stories

Pinskaje Paleśsie

Paternal-line core around Pahost-Zaharodzki — about three centuries in one place, a dense peasant and burgher network.

In search of free land

Sale of land in Kapiejnaje, Ihumeński paviet; move to Laby, Mienski paviet; purchase of land for all the sons of a large family.

In search of a runaway girl from Lithuania

This is about great-great-grandmother Hražyna. No parish books — we search through her descendants’ DNA. Matches converge on a geographic triangle in Lithuania. All parishes in the area are being checked.

In search of a better life. Immigration to the USA

I traced grandfather’s brother who left for the US in the early 1900s: passenger lists, census, Latin surname variants — the link restored after 70 years of silence.

From captivity to Sweden

After captivity in WWII the trail went cold. Through Swedish archives and displaced-persons lists he turned up as a resettler in Sweden.

Home from captivity

Andrej Ryhoravič went missing in World War I; in World War II he was taken captive, returned home and started a family.

Deported and fallen

Tracing relatives forced to Germany in WWII and those who never returned: Ostarbeiter lists, camps and military losses.

Were your ancestors from Belarus?

We specialise in genealogical research for Belarus and neighbouring historical territories — Lithuania, Poland, Ukraine and Latvia.

Cases where borders, administrative affiliation and place names changed more than once are especially relevant.

Why prices say “from”?

Genealogy cannot be priced only by how many people sit in a tree. Region, period, record survival, number of archives, search difficulty and branch count all matter. After we see your starting facts we propose a concrete plan and budget. We guarantee method and sources — not “N generations at any cost.”

FAQ

Before you start

What if parish registers did not survive?

That is not the end. We use indirect sources: revision lists, censuses, court and land files, relatives’ papers, military and other fonds. See also the article on missing parish books.

Do I need an exact birth date?

No. A name, approximate year, place and a few known relatives are often enough.

What if the surname is spelled differently?

Common in historical records. We account for spelling variants, document language, parish practice and period.

Can I start with one surname?

Yes — that is what the one-line package is for. Collateral branches can be added later by agreement.

Can I order research if I live abroad?

Yes. Most work is remote; deliverables are digital (report, documents, research log).

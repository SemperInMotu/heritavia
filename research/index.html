The offer

Research, scoped to the question

Heritavia takes a family question into the archives of Belarus and the former Grand Duchy lands, then returns a report that shows its working. There is no basket and no fixed package that promises a century, a number of ancestors, or a complete tree.

Choose the shape of the brief

One line

Line assessment

You name one line — usually the parent, grandparent, or emigrant whose origins are still unclear. Tell us the place-name as the family uses it, the religion if you know it, and what you have already tried.

The assessment says whether that line can be researched from here, which kind of record would have to exist, and what a first stage of work would cost. It is a professional look at the question, not a finished genealogy.

Broader research

A wider investigation

More than one line, a couple who met after a migration, siblings who left in different years, or a family story that has to be checked before anyone can say which line it belongs to.

The same opening: we read the brief and answer with a scope. Broader does not mean “as far as possible”. It means the question is larger than a single parent-to-child chain, and the fee follows that.

What the fee is, and is not

After the brief is clear, most investigations sit somewhere from about €1,000 to €10,000 or more. A short line with records already located is not the same job as several branches across a border that moved. Travel, copies, and translation of particular documents are discussed in the quote rather than hidden inside a slogan.

We do not sell a guaranteed depth. A parish book that burned is a fact about the archive, not a failure we can price our way past. If the honest answer is “this stage will not move the line”, that is the answer we send.

What you receive

  • A written scope before paid research begins, with the fee for that stage.
  • The searches we ran, including the ones that found nothing.
  • A report in which each claim is a record, an inference, or still open.
  • Copies or citations enough for someone else to check the work later.

DNA you already have can be read alongside the documents. Heritavia is not a laboratory and does not sell a test. A match list is not a parish.

The usual sequence

Brief

The form, or an email to info@heritavia.com. Correspondence can be in English, Russian, or Belarusian.

Assessment

We reply with what the question is really asking, and a fee for a defined stage. You decide whether to go on.

Report

Research against that scope. The handover is the report and its sources, described on the cases page.

If you want the context first — why these records are scattered, and how a proof is built — the guides are short and they are optional.

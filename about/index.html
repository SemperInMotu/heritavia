Heritavia

About

I’m Vitaly Khoruzhko. Heritavia is the practice that grew out of researching my own family in Polesie, and then doing the same kind of work for other people.

Where it started

The family had kept a map of land associated with Adaria Ivanovna Charužka in Pińsk Polesie, and a story connecting her with the local landowner Anton Tukałski-Nielubowicz. For a long time that was a legend I poked at in books. It became research when the legend had to survive contact with parish books, court papers, and the map itself.

The work

Systematic research started in the early 2010s: parish registers, revision lists, shifting borders and the languages on the page. Later, DNA matches were added as a separate kind of evidence — useful for a hypothesis, not a substitute for a record. The archives in ordinary use are NIAB in Minsk, LVIA in Vilnius, RGIA, and the regional archives in Belarus that now hold books written somewhere else.

What Heritavia is

A small practice in Minsk. You commission a defined stage of research. You receive a report that keeps records, inferences, and open questions apart. The public pages of this site are in English. The same person replies; correspondence can be in English, Russian, or Belarusian.

родословная.бел is the Belarusian and Russian site: a reading room in those languages, and the place to send an order if you want the form in Belarusian or Russian. It is not an English textbook, and these pages do not send you there to study.

Ask for the work

One line, or a wider brief. The fee comes after we have seen the question.
